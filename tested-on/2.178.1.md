# CineSense 2.178.1: what it was tested on

**Tested** means we ran it, and this page says on what, when, and with which version. **Expected to work** means we think it will and have no record of running it. Nothing on this page is a promise.

The automatic tests ran on 5 November 2026, on the exact code in this download.

## Your Mac

- Tested on **one Mac**: a MacBook Pro with an Apple M1 Max chip and 64 GB of memory, running **macOS 26.7.2**.
- Built for **Apple silicon** Macs. It is not built for Intel Macs.
- It asks for macOS 13 or later. **We have not run it on macOS 13, 14 or 15, or on any macOS but 26.7.2.** It may work there; we do not know.
- Other Macs and other Apple chips: not tested.

## Cards and drives

Copying to and from **FAT32, exFAT, Mac OS Extended and APFS** was tested.

Those tests ran on **disk images**. macOS mounts a disk image with the same file system drivers as a real card, but a disk image is not a physical card or a card reader.

Used for real, outside the tests, CineSense read cards formatted as exFAT and wrote to drives formatted as exFAT; the last time on 5 November 2026 with CineSense 2.178.1.

Not tested: NTFS drives, network drives, drives behind hubs or docks, and cards in readers other than the ones we own.

## DaVinci Resolve

Sending a day to Resolve was run for real in **DaVinci Resolve Studio 21.1.0.14**: 2 sends, the last on 5 November 2026 with CineSense 2.178.1.

The automatic tests send into **a model of Resolve built from measurements**, not into Resolve itself. 1380 of those checks ran, and none was skipped.

Other versions of Resolve: not tested.

The free version of Resolve cannot be controlled by other apps: not tested. Sending to Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro: **not supported**.

## Cloud copies (CloudSense)

Run for real against **Backblaze B2**, **Google Drive**, **Dropbox** and **Hetzner Storage Box (SFTP)**: 40 uploads in all, the last on 27 October 2026.

Not run against a real account: an SFTP server of your own; Amazon S3 and other S3 services.

The automatic tests use a stand-in for every service (rclone v1.75.1 sending to a folder on this Mac). They never touch a real account.

## How the automatic tests went

**80 results on the board, none failed.**

They are the engine's test suites, the app's own self-test (11544 checks), the Resolve colour check, the CineSearch suite and the tidy check.

In all, 21932 checks passed.

No check was skipped.

Run on 5 November 2026, on the Mac above.

## What was not tested

- macOS 13, 14 and 15; any macOS 26 other than 26.7.2; Intel Macs; other Apple chips
- NTFS drives, network drives, drives behind hubs and docks, physical card readers other than ours
- With a real card, as far as the records show: the cameras under Expected to work and under Test cards only
- Other versions of DaVinci Resolve
- The free version of DaVinci Resolve; Premiere Pro; Final Cut Pro
- An SFTP server of your own; Amazon S3 and other S3 services: with a real account
- CineSearch (a separate add-on; it gets its own list when it ships)
